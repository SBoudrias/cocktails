Sabrosa
shaken
crushed ice
tiki
  • Ingredients
  • ½ oz
    Lime juice
  • ½ oz
    Celery juice
  • ¾ oz
    Cream of coconut
  • ¼ oz
    John D. Taylor's Velvet Falernum
    Falernum liqueur
  • ½ oz
    Green Chartreuse
  • 1 ½ oz
    Siembra Valles Blanco
    Tequila (blanco)
​
Servings
  • Instructions
  • 1. Combine all the ingredients in a shaker and whip, shaking with a few pieces of crushed ice, just until incorporated.
  • 2. Dump into a tiki mug and fill the glass with crushed ice.
  • 3. Garnish with a mint bouquet. Serve with a straw.
