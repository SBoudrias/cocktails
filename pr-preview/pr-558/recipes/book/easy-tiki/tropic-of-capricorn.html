Tropic of Capricorn
shaken
up
coupe
  • Ingredients
  • ½ oz
    Cane syrup
  • ¾ oz
    Lime juice
  • 1 tsp
    Fernet-Branca
    Amaro (Fernet)
  • ¼ oz
    Tempus Fugit crème de banane
    Banana liqueur
  • 2 oz
    green tea–infused Blended aged rum (Demerara)
​
Servings
  • Instructions
  • 1. Combine all the ingredients in a cocktail shaker.
  • 2. Add ice and shake until chilled, about 10 seconds.
  • 3. Strain into a coupe or cocktail glass.

Now Easy Tiki is here to solve that problem!
