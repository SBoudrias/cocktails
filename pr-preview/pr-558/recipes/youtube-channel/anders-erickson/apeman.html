Apeman
shaken
up
coupe
  • Ingredients
  • 1
    Egg white
  • ¼ oz
    Demerara syrup
    66 Brix
  • ½ oz
    Lemon juice
  • ½ oz
    Lustau Don Nuño Oloroso Sherry
    Sherry (Oloroso)
  • ½ oz
    Giffard banane du Brésil
    Banana liqueur
  • 1 oz
    Old Grand-Dad Bonded Bourbon
    Whiskey (Bourbon)
​
Servings
  • Instructions
  • 1. Shake all ingredients without ice.
  • 2. Shake again with ice.
  • 3. Strain into a coupe glass.
  • 4. Garnish with expressed lemon oil and grated nutmeg.
