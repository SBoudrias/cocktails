Coconuts & Carnage

Coconuts & Carnage: Fifty Fiery Originals from the Emerald City is a battle-tested collection of 50 modern tropical cocktails by Seattle-based tiki bartender Justin Wojslaw, built on classic structure. From disciplined tributes to the tiki canon to originals that lean into fire and funk, every recipe hits hard and holds up in service.
