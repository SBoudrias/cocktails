Minimalist Tiki

Minimalist Tiki takes you on a fun four-part expedition through the ingredients and skills needed to achieve tiki drink mastery without buying hundreds of rums, syrups, and liqueurs. Also includes 130 recipes from bars including Pagan Idol, Dirty Dick, and Laki Kane, and bartenders including Marie King, Doc Parks, and Kevin Beary.
