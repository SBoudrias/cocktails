Frisco Club

  • up

Ingredients
  • 1
    Grapefruit peel
  • ¼ oz
    Lime juice
  • ½ oz
    Simple syrup
  • ¾ oz
    Grapefruit juice
  • ¼ oz
    Fernet-Branca
    Amaro (Fernet)
  • ½ oz
    Orange liqueur
  • 2 oz
    Plymouth Gin
    Gin (Plymouth)
Servings
Instructions
  • 1. Shake all the ingredients (except the grapefruit twist) with ice, then strain into a coupe. Squeeze the grapefruit twist over the drink and discard. No garnish.
