The Placebo
  • blended
  • blended
  • tiki
Ingredients
  • ¾ oz
    Lime juice
  • 2 oz
    Orange juice
  • 2 oz
    Cream of coconut
  • 6 oz
    Pineapple juice
  • 1 pinch
    Cinnamon
Servings
Instructions
  • 1. Add all ingredients to a blender with 1 cup of crushed ice.
  • 2. Blend until smooth.
  • 3. Pour into a pineapple-shaped glass.
  • 4. Garnish with grated nutmeg, pineapple fronds, and a tiki umbrella.
