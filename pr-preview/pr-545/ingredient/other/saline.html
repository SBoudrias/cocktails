Saline

Saline is a mixture of salt and water. Just like cooking, the addition of salt helps brighten up the flavor of a drink. For mixology use, it is usually composed of 20% salt and 80% water. You can substitute saline with a pinch of salt.

  • 1 part
    Salt
  • 4 part
    Water
