  • Instructions
  • 1. Grind the star anise, mace, nutmeg, and cloves using a mortar and pestle or spice grinder.
  • 2. Combine the lime peel, ginger, and rums in a blender. Blend on high speed until the ginger is liquefied and the lime peel is thoroughly diced.
  • 3. Combine the spices and blended mixture in a bag and vacuum seal it. Place in a 125 F water bath for 12 hours, or refrigerate for 24 hours.
  • 4. Fine-strain the mixture, pressing on the solids to maximize extraction, then add the almond extract, juices, and cane syrup. Combine with an equal amount of gum syrup.
  • 5. Bottle and refrigerate for up to 6 months. Makes 55 ounces.
